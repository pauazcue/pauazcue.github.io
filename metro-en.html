The context

Four media brands with completely different personalities, all under the same group.

MetroMedia is the group behind Metro 95.1, Infocampo, El Federal and Puls Urbano. Each brand has its own identity, audience and editorial logic — which makes visual communication a constant exercise in shifting register.

I worked at MetroMedia for three years, in charge of graphic and digital design across the group's brands. That's where I built my background in brand communication, campaigns and visual systems — before moving into digital product.

My role

Graphic design with responsibility over the group's visual communication.

My work ranged from campaign pieces and social media communication to visual identity, event materials and editorial design. At Metro 95.1 — the flagship brand — I also took part in conceptualizing advertising campaigns.

Working for a media group with such different audiences — from farmers reading Infocampo to Metro's young urban audience — pushed me to develop a flexible visual criteria and understand that style always serves the message.

What we built
→

Visual identity and campaigns for Metro 95.1

Graphic pieces, advertising campaigns and brand communication for one of the most listened-to radio stations in Buenos Aires.

→

Rebranding of group brands

Contributed to El Federal's visual identity redesign and adapted brand systems to new digital formats.

→

Editorial and digital design

Materials for Infocampo and Puls Urbano: newsletters, social media pieces, illustrations and digital content across different editorial contexts.

Working for brands with such different identities was a school of visual judgment. Every project meant stepping into a different universe and designing from the inside — not imposing a style, but finding each brand's own.
