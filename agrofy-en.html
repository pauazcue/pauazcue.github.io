The context

Argentina's agricultural supply market, brought into a digital marketplace.

Agrofy is Latin America's largest agricultural supply marketplace. It connects rural producers with suppliers of seeds, agrochemicals, machinery and services — a historically fragmented market, with long buying cycles and high-value decisions.

I worked at Agrofy for two years, on a growing product team. The challenge was translating complex purchasing logic — which in the physical world depends on years-long business relationships — into a digital experience that built trust and simplified the process.

My role

Product design on a growing team.

I worked as a Product Designer on catalog, geolocation, lead capture and editorial content initiatives. I took part in discovery processes, user research and hypothesis validation alongside Product and Data.

The team was small and the pace was fast. A big part of the work was finding the balance between speed of delivery and depth of research — and knowing when each one mattered more.

What we built
→

Search and catalog experience

Redesigned the product listing and search experience to improve conversion and the clarity of the available offer.

→

Supplier geolocation

Feature to connect producers with local distributors, accounting for the particularities of the Argentine rural market.

→

Research in real context

Research with rural producers at agricultural trade shows — one of the few places where the product's actual users were directly reachable.

Designing for the field meant understanding a universe of decisions unlike urban e-commerce. Users weren't impulsive buyers — they were producers who spent weeks evaluating before closing a deal.
